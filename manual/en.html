Welcome

Capturia is your digital fishing log: record your fishing spots, your catches (with photos, weight and the day's conditions) and also those days when nothing bit. Everything is stored on your phone and, if you like, backed up to the cloud. Over time it becomes your own fishing history: which species bites best at which spot, with which tide, with which wind.

This guide grows little by little as the app grows.

Getting started

  1. When you open the app you choose the language (your phone's is suggested; you can change it later in Settings) and whether you already have an account or are new. If you already have an account, you sign in with Continue with Google or with your email and go straight to Home.
  2. If you are new, pick your fishing area by hand (continent, country and, in Spain, region), where you usually fish and the look of the app (light or dark, which you can change whenever you like in Settings).
  3. Choose your angler type: shore, river, boat or spearfishing. The catch form adapts to the way you fish (see My profile).
  4. Choose your username: the app checks right away whether it is free. Once set, it can no longer be changed. On the same screen you enter your country and city and whether you want other anglers to find you. Then you create your account with Google or with your email.
  5. Your catalogue starts with five species already loaded: Gilthead bream, Sea bass, Squid, Octopus and Salmon.
  6. You start on the Free plan. Your plan is in the avatar menu > Subscription and plan.

Getting around the app

  • Home: at the top, today's forecast for your main spot and the Learn how to use Capturia and What fish is it? buttons (hide them in Settings > Top buttons). Below, a grid of shortcuts to what you use most; the ones your plan doesn't include show a padlock. With Premium you can choose which shortcuts you see and in what order: press and hold one, or go to Settings > Home screen layout.
  • Bottom bar: Species, Spots, Catches, Blanks, Calendar and Hallelujah! (new catch). The button of the section you're in is marked with a darker border and a lighter background.
  • Side menu (☰), in collapsible sections (only one open at a time):
    • Your links: your favourite websites, one tap away (Free 3, Plus 5 and Premium 8).
    • My gear: rods, reels and lines, baits and lures, and other.
    • Backup: save and restore your fishing data in the cloud (from Plus).
    • Catches and species: Public catches, Advanced statistics, What fish is it?, Import my catches and the Public species list.
    • Planning: Fishing forecast, Weather forecast, Tide tables and My alerts.
    • Learning: step-by-step knots and fishing guides.
    • Community: My buddies, Messages, View groups, Find groups, Create groups and Meetups.
    • Help: FAQ, this manual and About Capturia.
    Options from another plan show their label (Plus or Premium) and, when you tap them, tell you which plan includes them. You can change the order of the sections in Settings > Side menu order. At the top there's a shortcut to 🏠 Home.
  • Avatar menu (your photo, top right): 🏠 Home, My profile, Settings, Subscription & plan, My documents, My annotations, About Capturia and Log out.

My profile

Your profile is your card in Capturia. It's in the avatar menu (your photo, top right) > My profile.

  • Photo: tap it to take one with the camera, pick one from the gallery or delete it. Below it you see your current plan.
  • Email and username: your email is hidden; tap it and confirm with your fingerprint, Face ID or phone lock to see it. Your username is unique, it's how others find you and it can't be changed.
  • Angler type: tap your main profile (shore, river, boat or spearfishing), which your new catches start with, and tick below the other ways you fish so you can choose them on each catch. It's saved instantly. If you change the main one, the previous one moves to “other ways”.
  • My area: your country and your city or town. At the bottom of the card, the Visible to other users switch decides whether others can find you from Buddies > Search by area.
  • Delete my account: at the bottom of the profile. To confirm you type DELETE. Your account and everything you have in the cloud are deleted forever.

Spots

A spot is a place where you fish: a reservoir, a stretch of river, a beach. From Spots, tap + New and fill in:

  • Name and spot type (reservoir, river, sea, etc.).
  • Photo, optional.
  • Location: your current GPS position or a point you mark on the map.
  • Visibility: public or private. A public spot can be seen by anyone who receives a catch of yours made there; a private one cannot.

Each spot shows how many catches and blanks you have recorded there. Tap the card to open View spot: at the top you have Edit, Delete and ✈️ Send (Premium plan); at the bottom, the coordinates and Open in maps to get there. You can't have two spots with the same name: if you repeat one, the app asks you to change it. If you delete a spot that has catches, blanks or big bites, they are not deleted: they are left without a spot, marked as pending, and you can assign them another one later. With the magnifying glass, next to "All spots", you search a spot by name as you type.

Your own types ★ (from the Plus plan): if none of Capturia's types fits, tap + New when choosing the type and write your own (for example, “Breakwater”). It is marked with ★ next to the usual ones, saved with your data and in your cloud copy, and counted in the statistics. With My spot types, in the same picker, you rename or delete them; if you delete one, the spots using it switch to “Other”. When you send a spot to a buddy, its custom type reaches them by name.

Import from Google Maps: in Google Maps, press and hold the point where you fish (or open a place), tap Share and choose Capturia. New spot opens with the name and location already filled in: choose the type and tap Create spot. From the spot form you can also pick one of your places saved in Maps. This way you can save spots you've seen on the map without having to go there.

Catches

From Catches, tap +🎣, next to the search bar (or Hallelujah! in the bottom bar), to record a fish. You can add:

  • Species and spot (required), weight and length.
  • Mode: each new catch starts with the main profile from My profile; if you ticked other ways of fishing, you can change it for that catch at the top. The techniques and details change with the mode:
    • Shore: shore techniques (surfcasting, spinning, rockfishing…), depth and casting distance.
    • River: no tide or waves; instead, the Water conditions card (type of stretch, flow and water temperature).
    • Boat: boat techniques (anchored, drifting, trolling, jigging…), depth, water temperature and the status of your on-board documents according to My documents. In Spain there is also the Documents and PescaREC card: inland or outer waters, whether it was in a marine reserve and whether you have already declared it in PescaREC.
    • Spearfishing: no bait, lure or “Released” box; you note the depth, visibility, water temperature, current and whether you went in from the shore or from a boat.
    In every mode except spearfishing you can tick Released if you returned the fish to the water. Changing the mode clears the chosen technique.
  • Minimum size: once you’ve chosen the species and spot, below the length you’ll see the minimum size for that area, in red if your fish is below it (see Species).
  • Photos or video of the catch.
  • Multiple catch: if you caught several fish at once, record them as a single entry; in that case you enter the quantity instead of the length.
  • Lure and bait (below the quantity or length): pick the ones you used from your Equipment list. They are optional; if you haven't created any yet, the field tells you to do so in Equipment.
  • Day's conditions: with the weather button, from the spot or your location, the weather (wind, rain, cloud cover and moon phase) and the sea (sea state, swell and its direction, wave period, tide and tide type) are filled in automatically. You note water clarity and colour yourself, and you can correct any value. Everything is optional, but the more you fill in, the more useful your history becomes over time.
  • Annotations (at the end, before visibility): anything you want to remember about that catch (how it bit, the rig, what you'd change…). It's always private: only you see it, even if the catch is visible to your buddies or goes on the map, and it isn't included when you send or share the catch. It also appears in My annotations.
  • Visibility, with four levels you can change whenever you like:
    • Private: only you see it. Every new catch is created like this.
    • Buddies: the buddies in My buddies see it. They see the photo, the species and the weight; when you choose it, Capturia asks what else you want to show (date and time, spot, length, weather and sea, bait and lure, other photos).
    • Public (map): added anonymously to the Public catches map; nobody knows it's yours and your buddies don't see it. Only the species, weight, place, date and time are sent, without photos or video.
    • Buddies and public: both at once.
    To go on the map, the catch needs a spot with a location, date, time and weight; if something is missing, Capturia tells you what. Catches for buddies or for the map are uploaded to the cloud even if you don't have automatic upload.

The bar at the top of Catches has two rows. In the first you filter by species or spot, and with + Filters you get advanced filters (dates, weight, catch type, lure, bait and specific conditions), available from the Premium plan. In the second you have the search bar, which filters by species name as you type, the +🎣 button to record a catch and the 👥🎣 button for your buddies' catches.

Your buddies' catches are never mixed with yours, so you can show your list to anyone. To see them, tap 👥🎣: its border turns red and the list shows only your buddies' catches. The ones they sent you and you saved have a red border on the left; your buddies' public ones (from Premium) have a light blue border and the name of who recorded them. Tap the button again to see yours. Buddies' catches can be viewed (and the received ones deleted), but not edited or forwarded.

On your catches, 👥 Shared with buddies means your buddies can see it (Buddies or Buddies and public visibility) and ✅ Posted on the map means it appears on the public catches map. Among your buddies' catches, public ones show Posted by and the name of who logged it, and received ones show Sent by. If a buddy sent you a catch they have also posted, it appears only once, with “Sent by … · also posted”. In + Filters, the 👥 Fishing buddies card lets you tick which buddies' catches you want to see; with none ticked, you see everyone's. When you send a catch, it goes with its lure (and its type, even a custom one).

Share on social media

Show off your catches on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or wherever you like with an image prepared by Capturia. In the list, press and hold the catch's card (or tap ⋮) and choose Share; inside a catch, use the share button at the top, next to Send. You choose how the image looks:

  • Photo only: as it is, with a small Capturia mark in one corner. From inside the catch, all its photos are shared.
  • Photo with my details: the cover photo with a band below showing the species, weight, length and date, the verified photo badge if it has one and a QR code to download Capturia.
  • Story: the same card in portrait, for stories and statuses. You can change the top text (“Fish on! 🎣”) or leave it empty.

The spot only appears if you tick it: your spot is yours. The “Made with Capturia” mark and the QR code are always included; with Premium you can remove them. The images go out without the photo's GPS location, and anyone who sees them can scan the QR code to reach capturia.es.

Verified photos ✅

When you add a photo to a catch, Capturia asks whether you want to take the photo now or choose it from the gallery. Photos you take at that moment with the camera from Capturia are verified: they carry the ✅ badge in the catch and the card shows “Verified photo”. Gallery photos (and those from Import from my gallery) do not get the badge, because they may come from anywhere or be edited. If you want your buddies to know your fish is real, take the photo from Capturia.

Viewing, editing or deleting a catch

Tap a card to open View catch. At the top you have Edit, Delete and the send icon. When you tap Edit, "Edit" becomes Save (enabled as soon as you change something, including photos, the cover or the video) and "Delete" becomes Cancel (discards the changes). Species and Spots work the same way.

When deleting, the "Also delete from the cloud" box (ticked by default) decides whether your cloud copy is deleted too, not just the one on your phone. If you are offline at that moment, the app warns you and does it by itself as soon as you are back online or reopen the app. The same applies to Spots, Species, Blanks and Big bites.

Delete several at once: long-press a card (or tap ⋮) and choose Delete several, the last option in the menu. The screen switches to selection mode with that card already ticked: tap any others you want, use All to tick or untick them all and press the red 🗑 button with the number. You confirm once, with the same cloud checkbox, and they are deleted with the same rules as one by one. Cancel leaves selection mode. It is available in Catches, Species (with the “Also delete their catches” checkbox: if you leave it unticked, species with catches are not deleted), Spots, Blanks, Baits and lures and the rest of Equipment.

Import my catches

Do you have photos in your gallery of fish from before you used Capturia? In the menu (☰) > Catches and species > 🖼️ Import my catches (or with Share → Capturia from the gallery) you pick the photos and the app groups them into catches by the time they were taken; before importing you can split or merge them. On Android you can also pick a whole folder. Imported catches come in without a spot and marked as pending (yellow border in the list) until you complete them. They don't carry the ✅ badge and they follow your plan's limits.

Blanks and big bites

Not everything is catches: in Blanks you also keep the days when nothing bit. A blank records the date, the spot, the fishing hours and the day's conditions, just like a catch but without a fish. Over time it helps you learn which conditions do not work at each spot.

A big bite is the one that got away: you felt the pull or your line broke, but the fish never came out. You can note the species you suspect it was and whether the line broke. Both are recorded from the same screen, with the big-bite switch, and the list is filtered with All / Blanks / Bites.

In a blank you can set the species you were after; if you do not choose one, it stays as All. At the top of the list, next to the Spots filter, there is a Species filter: it shows the blanks for that species (and those set to «All») and the big bites where you marked it as a suspect. To record a new one, tap the + 👎 button.

Calendar

The Calendar button in the bottom bar (between Blanks and Hallelujah!) opens a monthly view with a coloured dot for every day you recorded something: catches, blanks and big bites. Tap a day to see what you noted and open that record read-only.

Species

The Species screen shows your catalogue: the species on your phone (the five you start with, the ones you add from the public list and the ones you create yourself). Each card shows the photo, the type, how many catches you have and the minimum size for your area. It syncs with your cloud copy manually or automatically, depending on Settings.

Use the magnifying glass, next to “All species”, to search by common or scientific name. Tap a card to open View species: at the top you have Edit, Delete and the icon to send it to a buddy. Deleting a species shows the same notice as Spots and Catches, with the option to delete it from the cloud too. If it has catches, they are deleted with it: the app tells you how many and asks you to type the confirmation word.

Public species list (official catalogue)

This is Capturia’s official catalogue, reviewed by the team and with a photo of each species. It’s in the menu (☰) > Catches and species > 🐟 Public species list, from the Plus plan.

  • Area: the list is filtered by area (European Atlantic, Cantabrian Sea, Mediterranean…). Yours is selected when you open it; you can pick another one or “All zones”. You can also search by name and filter by type.
  • More information: tap a species’ name (or the ▾ arrow) to expand its card, with a large photo, the name, the scientific name, the type, where it lives and the minimum size. 📖 In depth opens its full profile: key facts, how to recognise it, where it lives, what it eats, how it breeds, how to fish for it and a fun fact. If it has no profile yet, the button is dimmed.
  • Add to your catalogue: tap ⊕. You get your own copy, which you can edit or remove whenever you like. Species you already have don’t appear in the list; turn on “Also show the ones I already have” to see them marked with ✓.

Official species or your own? If the species is in the public list, add it from there: it comes with its photo, its name in your language, its scientific name and its minimum sizes by area, kept up to date by the Capturia team. Only create one yourself if it isn’t there; if you make it public, you propose it for the catalogue and the team reviews it. When you choose an official species in a catch (also with What fish is it?), it’s added to your catalogue automatically, without duplicates if you already had it. If the catalogue changes the photo of a species you have, editing it offers Use the official photo: your photo is never changed unless you choose to.

If you create a species by hand and its name matches one in the official catalogue, the app warns you: Use the official one adds it to your species with its photo, sizes and fact sheet, and Create mine carries on with yours. If you propose as public a species that was already in the catalogue, the team will let you know instead of verifying it.

Minimum size for your area

The same species can have different minimum sizes depending on where it’s caught (for example, the axillary seabream: 17 cm in general and 25 cm in the Cantabrian Sea). That’s why official species have a size for each area, and the In depth profile shows all of them, with yours highlighted.

  • Your area comes from the country and region you chose when you started. Change it in Settings > My fishing area.
  • In a catch, below the length you’ll see the minimum size for the chosen spot (its area is worked out from the spot’s location). If the length or weight is below it, the notice turns red.
  • The area is worked out on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

Sizes are a guide only: always check the current regulations for your area. Species you create yourself have a single size, the one you type.

How many species you can have in your catalogue depends on your plan (see Plans). Official catalogue names are shown in your language when a translation exists; species you create are shown as you wrote them.

What fish is it? AI identification

Caught a fish and don't know what it is? Take a photo and the AI tells you which species it is. The tool is on Home (the What fish is it? button, which you can hide in Settings > Top buttons), in the menu (☰) and inside New catch and New species, next to the species field.

Take a photo with the camera or pick one from your gallery and tap Identify species. In a few seconds you see up to 3 possible species, from most to least likely, with the % confidence, your photo next to the official catalogue one, the features it looks at and the approximate minimum size for your area (always check it against current regulations). For better results: the whole fish, side-on, in good light, with the fins clearly visible.

From a catch, That's it fills in the species and, if it is from the official catalogue, adds it to your species. When creating a species, Use this species fills in the name, scientific name and type. From the standalone tool you can share the result with a friend. If you're not convinced, Try Google Lens gives you a free second opinion.

Identifications per month: Free 2, Plus 10 and Premium 50. The counter resets every month and, if an identification fails because of an error, it isn't counted. The photo is sent reduced and without hidden data (EXIF) only to analyse it, and it isn't stored; for the minimum size your approximate area is used, never your exact location. It needs an internet connection.

Advanced statistics

Advanced statistics (Premium only) analyse your catches to tell you when and in which conditions you catch the most. They are in the menu (☰) > Catches and species > 📊 Advanced statistics.

At the top you choose the species (all or one) and the period (last year, last 3 years or all). The summary shows the number of catches, different spots, average weight and heaviest weight, and When they bite most tells you what repeats most often (time, moon, tide, wind…) when there are at least 3 catches with that data.

Below you have catches by month (the best month highlighted), by time of day (morning 6–14 h, afternoon 14–21 h, night 21–6 h) and two collapsible sections for weather and sea: wind, rain, clouds, moon phase, sea state, swell, wave period, tide, water clarity and colour. The more data you record in your catches, the more accurate they get. All users mode shows statistics for everyone's public catches around your location, a point on the map or one of your spots (10 to 100 km, official catalogue species only). The data is anonymous, with no names or exact points, and you need to contribute at least 3 public catches to see it.

By spot type and By lure type show how many catches you have and their average weight for each type (beach, rocks, harbour…; soft plastic, jig, minnow…), including your own types ★. Only catches with a lure count in the lure one.

Public catches

A map of the public catches of every Capturia angler, grouped by area: which species are caught in each area, how many and at what average weight. It's in the menu (☰) > Catches and species > 🗺️ Public catches and it's a Premium feature.

  • To see it you need to have contributed at least 3 public catches: set a catch's visibility to Public (map) or Buddies and public. It needs a spot with a location, date, time and weight. Any plan can contribute.
  • Period: month, 3 months, year or all. Area size: 10, 25, 50 or 100 km.
  • Tap an area to see the total and, by species, the number of catches and the average weight.

It's always anonymous: only areas with at least 3 catches from 2 different anglers are shown, and nobody ever sees who caught what or the exact spot. When you contribute a catch, Capturia keeps an anonymous copy (species, weight, place, date and time, no photos) even if you later delete it or make it private.

Forecast, weather and tides

The Planning section of the menu (☰) helps you decide when and where to go. You need spots with a location (GPS or map).

🔮 Fishing forecast

It scores each hour from 0 to 100 at your spots by combining solunar periods, sunrise and sunset, moonrise and moonset, the moon phase, pressure, wind and, at sea, tides and waves. At the top you choose the spot and the day (each day with its stars, from 1 to 5); the chart has one bar per hour and, when you tap it, explains what adds and what subtracts. Below are up to 3 highlighted time windows and, at sea, the high and low tides. With Premium the forecast also learns from your catch history at that spot. The last spot you check becomes your main spot: it appears on the Home card and is the one used for your best-hour alert (switch at the bottom of the screen).

🌦️ Weather forecast

Plan your session: choose the spot, day, start time and duration, and you'll see a summary (average wind and gusts, waves, water temperature), danger warnings (thunderstorm, strong gusts, rough sea, swell, fog), the rain with the longest dry window, the tides that fall within your session, the weather hour by hour and how the pressure changes. Beyond 5 days it's only a trend. Weather and sea data come from Stormglass.

🌊 Tide tables

For your sea spots: each high and low tide with its approximate time to the minute and its height, plus the day's range and curve. If the pressure is low or high, the table tells you how many centimetres higher or lower the sea will be. These are the tides of the nearest station (Stormglass) and, on coasts with tides, you also see the coefficient, calculated from the Brest tides as in the official tables. They are approximate: they don't replace the official port tables for navigation.

Forecast days: Plus 4 and Premium 8 (fishing, weather and tides). With Free you can see what each screen offers and its plan.

Condition alerts

Tell Capturia what sea, weather and tide you're looking for at one of your spots and it will send you a notification, even with the app closed, when the forecast matches. They're in the menu (☰) > Planning > 🔔 My alerts and on each spot with a location (Condition alerts card). They're a Premium feature: up to 3 active at a time.

  • Conditions (choose only the ones you care about): wave height and period, swell direction, sky (clear or cloudy), rain (none, light or heavy), maximum wind and tide (rising, falling, high or low tide ±1 h). Rivers, reservoirs and lakes have no sea data.
  • Before and after: before a sharp pressure change (3, 4 or 6 hPa in the next 6 hours), after a storm (several days of big waves that ended yesterday or the day before) or after several days of rain.
  • Time window (optional), minimum duration in a row and how far ahead to check the forecast: today, until tomorrow or the next 3 days.

We check the forecast every 6 hours and alert you only once per alert and day; tap the notification to see the time window, why it's met and the hour-by-hour data. Each alert lasts 1 month: a few days before it ends we remind you to renew it with one tap. You can pause it whenever you like; paused or expired alerts don't count towards the limit. To check it we store the spot's name and coordinates on our server, and they're deleted when you delete the alert.

Knots and fishing guides

In the menu (☰) > Learning, for every plan and offline:

  • 🪢 Knots: the most common ones, grouped by use (tying the hook, joining lines, loops, and lures and spade-end hooks), each drawn step by step. The braid is blue and the leader orange.
  • Fishing guides: Gear (hooks, rods, reels and accessories), Baits and lures (natural baits, hard and soft lures) and Getting ready (how to read forecasts). They keep growing little by little.

On Home, Learn how to use Capturia also has visual guides for every part of the app.

Equipment

In the side menu (☰), the My gear section gathers what you take fishing: baits and lures, rods, reels and lines, and Other.

Baits and lures

Here you keep your own catalogue of baits (worms, sardine, squid…) and lures (soft plastics, minnows, jigs…). The button at the top switches the filter between All, Lures and Baits, and next to it + New creates one:

  • Choose whether it is a lure or a bait (this can't be changed later).
  • Name and description (colour, size, weight, how you use it…).
  • For a lure, its type (popper, soft plastic, spoon, jig, minnow…), optional.
  • Photo, optional.

Each card shows the photo, name, description and in how many catches you've used it. When you record a catch, pick the lure and/or bait you caught it with (both optional) and, over time, you'll see which one works best for you. If you delete one you've already used, your catches are not deleted: they simply no longer have that bait or lure.

Limits: 5 baits and lures in total on Free, 100 on Plus and unlimited on Premium.

Your own lure types ★ (from the Plus plan): if your lure doesn't fit any type, tap + New in the type picker and create your own (for example, “Squid jig”). With My lure types you rename or delete them; if you delete one, the lures using it switch to “Other”, which is also among Capturia's types.

My documents

Store your fishing licence, insurance or permits so you always have them at hand, even without signal. They're in the avatar menu (your photo, top right) > 🪪 My documents.

  • Type (sea, freshwater or federation licence, insurance, fishing-area permit, boat papers, boating licence or other), name, number, issuer, area, issue and expiry dates, linked spot, notes and up to 4 photos.
  • Expiry: each document shows whether it's valid, expiring soon or already expired. We remind you 30 days before, 7 days before and on the day itself, at 10:00. If a permit linked to a spot has expired, we remind you when you log a catch there.
  • Lock: turn on "Ask for fingerprint or PIN to open" and your phone's fingerprint, face or PIN will be required to open them.

Your documents and their photos are stored only on this phone: they aren't uploaded to the cloud, aren't included in the backup and can't be shared. If you uninstall the app or change phones, they're lost. Limit: Free 2, Plus 10 and Premium unlimited.

My annotations

Your fishing notebook: jot down anything you want to remember (rigs, ideas, what another angler told you, what to try next time…). It's in the avatar menu (your photo, top right) > 📝 My annotations, on every plan.

  • + New creates an annotation with an optional title and the text. Tap one to view it: at the top you have Edit and Delete.
  • Your catch annotations (the Annotations field of the form) also appear here, with the species and date; from the annotation you can open the catch. If you delete the catch, its annotation is deleted with it.
  • At the top you search by any word and filter between All, Standalone and Catches.

Annotations are always private: your buddies don't see them, they aren't sent, they don't go on the Public catches map and they don't appear when you share. They're saved on your phone and, if you have backup (from Plus), in your cloud copy too, so you get them back on a new phone.

Buddies

From the Premium plan you can add buddies (side menu > My buddies, searching by username) and send them, with the Send button on each record, catches with all their information (including weather and sea data), spots, species and big bites. Users on Plus or higher can receive them.

  • When you receive something, the app tells you when you open it (or right away, if it is already open). You can save it or discard it. If you already have that species or spot, yours is reused instead of creating a duplicate.
  • When someone adds you as a buddy, you get a notice right away (even with the app open), with the option to Add them too if your plan allows it.
  • Pending requests: at the very top of My buddies you always have the requests card. Under Received, people who have added you and you haven't answered yet: accept (and, if your plan allows it, add them too) or decline (you leave their list; it isn't a block). Under Sent, people you've added who haven't accepted you yet; until then they don't receive what you send, and you can cancel the request. In your list, anyone who hasn't accepted you yet has the ⏳ Pending label. If you're not Premium, received requests are in Community > Buddy requests.
  • Search by area: below the search bar, tap Search by area, choose the country and, if you like, type the city. You see the anglers in that area who have filled in My area and have “Visible to other users” turned on. Fill in yours so they can find you too.
  • Hide from public search by turning off “Visible to other users” in My profile (at the bottom of the My area card) if you want to use Capturia just as a fishing log: nobody will be able to find you to add you.
  • Remove (X): they stop being your buddy.
  • Block (padlock, under the X): besides removing them, they can no longer add you or send you anything. Items they had not accepted from you yet are cancelled, and the catches and big bites they already accepted are deleted from their cloud and from their phone the next time they open the app. If someone sends you something you don't want, you can also block and report them from the notice itself.
  • Unblock: in the expandable "Blocked users" card. They don't become your buddy again automatically, and what was deleted when blocking is not recovered.

What you share: in My buddies, each row has a share icon. Tapping it shows two tabs: You share, your catches with Buddies or Buddies and public visibility (all your buddies see them), and Shares with you, the ones that buddy lets their buddies see. What you have sent each other directly still appears when you tap the row.

Messages

A message is a one-off notice, without opening a chat: your phone number (contact card), a short text or a meeting point on the map. They're in the menu (☰) > Community > ✉️ Messages, and from Plus you can send and receive them. The same message can go to several buddies at once (up to 5 with Plus and up to 50 with Premium) or to a whole group from its chat. When you get one, you can reply, call, write on WhatsApp, open the point on the map or remove it. In Sent messages you see who has read it. If there's no connection, the message stays pending and is sent automatically when you're back online. Don't confuse them with My annotations, which are personal notes only you can see.

Groups

Groups are chats between anglers. From the Plus plan you can see your groups, find public groups (filtering by water: fresh, salt or general, and by area) and join them, up to 2 public and 2 private at a time. From Premium you can create groups (up to 3, with up to 50 members), public or private, and invite your buddies first and then anyone you search for by username. The group's creator can moderate the chat and delete messages. Catches, spots and species shared in the chat are only shown in full to Premium users.

Meetups

A meetup is a fishing plan with a date, time, place (or your location) and a comment. From Premium you create it in the menu (☰) > Community > 📍 Meetups and invite your buddies; from Plus you can receive invitations and reply I'm in or Can't make it. In the list you see who's going (✓), who isn't (✕) and who hasn't replied (?). You can change your answer and the organiser can cancel it. Meetups are deleted automatically 3 days after their date.

Settings

From your avatar menu you open Settings, where you can change:

  • The app's language and theme (light or dark).
  • Colour customisation: colour of the header and bottom bar, bar icons in white or black (all plans; in dark mode they turn white automatically) and card colour (from Plus). "Restore original colours" brings back the defaults.
  • App background: preset backgrounds or a colour (from Plus), or your own photo from the camera or gallery (from Premium). Preset backgrounds come from Pixabay.
  • Top buttons: what appears at the top of Home (today's forecast, Learn how to use Capturia and What fish is it?).
  • Home screen layout (Premium): which shortcuts appear on Home and in what order; Reset brings back the usual grid. It's saved on this phone.
  • Side menu order: drag the side menu sections into the order you want.
  • Card actions: if you want to see the pencil and bin again in the lists, or the send and share icons.
  • Automatic upload to the cloud (from Plus). If you don't turn it on, you can save and restore your copy manually from the side menu > Backup or from Home.
  • My fishing area: the area used to show minimum sizes. By default it's "Automatic", based on the country and region you chose when you started. It's only saved on this phone.

Notifications: they have their own card in Settings (tap it to expand it), with a main switch and, below it, one for each type of alert. Everything is on by default. Your phone alerts you even when Capturia is closed when your best fishing hour starts (days with 3★ or more at your main Forecast spot) and, from Plus, when a buddy sends you something (catch, spot, species or big bite), you get a private message or a meetup, someone adds you as a buddy or invites you to a group, one of your species is reviewed or, with Premium, one of your alerts is met or about to expire. From Plus you choose which of these alerts you want to get; that choice is saved in your account and applies to all your phones. If you turn off the main switch, you get no alerts at all, not even the best-hour one. When you log out, that phone stops receiving them. Expiry reminders for My documents come from the phone itself.

Frequently asked questions

Do I lose my data if I delete the app or change phones? No, as long as you have your copy in the cloud: your data is saved on your phone first and, with the backup, in the cloud too. On a new phone, sign in and use Restore fishing data (side menu > Backup, or the Home button).

If I delete a catch that is already in the cloud, is it deleted there too? Yes, if you leave the "Also delete from the cloud" box ticked when confirming the deletion.

Can I use Capturia offline? Yes, to record and look up your data. Syncing with the cloud (uploads, backups and pending deletions) happens by itself as soon as you are back online.

What does the ✅ on a photo mean? That the photo was taken with the camera from Capturia when the catch was recorded, not uploaded from the gallery. It is not absolute proof, but it is a good sign that the photo is genuine.
