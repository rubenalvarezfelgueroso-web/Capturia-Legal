This policy explains what information Capturia (the fishing app available for Android/iOS) collects, how we use it and what rights you have over it. It has been drafted in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and the Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD).

This policy is available in several languages. In the event of any discrepancy between versions, the Spanish version shall prevail.

1. Data controller

Rubén Álvarez Felgueroso, developer of Capturia, decides what data is processed and for what purpose.
Contact: capturiaSpain@gmail.com

As this is an app by an individual developer, there is no Data Protection Officer (one is not required for this type of processing).

2. What data we process

Account and profile

  • Registration: email address and password, or your Google or Apple account if you choose "Sign in with Google" or "Sign in with Apple". If you choose to hide your email when signing in with Apple, we only receive the anonymous relay address Apple assigns you, not your real email. We never see your password: it is managed in encrypted form by our authentication provider, Supabase.
  • Public profile: username, avatar photo, a short description and the links you choose to add yourself in "My links".
  • Subscription plan (Free/Plus/Premium/Pro) and the platform you use the app on (Android/iOS/web), so we can provide support and for internal usage statistics.

Content you create in the app

  • Fishing spots: name, type, photo and GPS coordinates of the place (only if you enable location when creating a spot).
  • Catches: species, spot, photos of the fish, a video if you decide to add one, and environmental data of the moment (tide, moon phase, wind, sea state, etc.) that you enter yourself.
  • Blank days and big bites: records of your fishing sessions (with or without a catch), including the description you write.
  • Your own species: if you add a species that is not in the official catalogue, including its photo.

All this content can be marked as private or public; public content is visible to other Capturia users.

Technical data

  • Chosen language and theme (light/dark), and the visual customisation preferences available from certain plans.
  • Internal identifier of your account, used to link it with the payment provider (RevenueCat) and the database.

Capturia does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not access your contacts, messages or other apps on your device.

4. Where your data is stored

By default, on the Free plan, your species, spots, catches and blank days are stored only on your device (local database) and never reach our servers.

From the Plus plan, if you enable cloud backup, that data (in addition to your profile and account, which exist from registration) is stored on Supabase servers located in the United Kingdom (London).

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data to anyone. We only share it with the providers strictly necessary for the app to work, who act as data processors:

  • Supabase (database, authentication and storage of photos and videos).
  • RevenueCat (management of subscriptions and in-app purchases; a company based in the United States).
  • Google or Apple, only if you choose to sign in with your Google or Apple account.
  • Apple / Google (App Store / Google Play), as distribution platforms and for managing subscription payments.

When these providers are outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is carried out under the safeguards provided for in the GDPR. Specifically, our database (Supabase) is hosted in the United Kingdom, a country that has an adequacy decision from the European Commission: this means it is considered to offer a level of protection equivalent to that of the EU, so data can be transferred there without additional safeguards. For the other providers outside the EU/EEA (such as RevenueCat, in the United States), the transfer is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your profile and the content associated with it from our servers, unless the law requires us to keep something (for example, for accounting reasons or dispute resolution) for the legally required period.

You can delete your account from within the app (My profile → Delete my account) or by email. How to delete your account.

7. Your rights

As a Capturia user, you can exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access: find out what data of yours we process.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data (much of it, such as your display name or photo, you can change yourself in "My profile" and "Settings").
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data.
  • Portability: receive your data in a reusable format.
  • Objection and restriction: object to a specific processing or ask us to restrict it.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at capturiaSpain@gmail.com. We will reply within one month at most.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the EU or EEA country where you live, work or where the alleged infringement took place. For example: in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission (DPC); in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO); in Spain, where Capturia’s controller is established, the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). You can find the authorities of every country on the European Data Protection Board website.

8. Minors

Capturia is not intended for children under 14. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a minor in your care has provided us with personal data without your authorisation, contact us so we can delete it.

9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data: authentication and cloud storage are handled through Supabase, with encrypted connections (HTTPS) and row-level access control (each user can only read and modify their own data, except content that others mark as public).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our practices or the applicable regulations change. If the change is significant, we will notify you within the app itself. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For any questions about this policy or your data, write to us at capturiaSpain@gmail.com.
