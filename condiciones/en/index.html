These terms apply to purchases made inside Capturia (the fishing app for Android/iOS): the Plus plan (one-time payment) and the Premium subscription. By purchasing you accept these terms, in addition to those of the store (Google Play or App Store) you pay through.

These terms are available in several languages. In case of discrepancy between versions, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Who sells to you

Capturia is developed by Rubén Álvarez Felgueroso (Capturia Spain), who you can contact at capturiaSpain@gmail.com.

Payment is always taken by the store you buy from (Google Play or App Store), using the payment method on your store account. Capturia never receives or stores your card details.

2. Plans and prices

  • Free: no cost.
  • Plus: one-time payment. Unlocks the Plus features with no recurring payments.
  • Premium: monthly subscription with automatic renewal. You need to have bought Plus to subscribe (being on the free Plus trial is not enough).
  • Pro: status for professionals, granted on request by email; it is not purchased in the app.

The price you pay is the one shown by the store at the time of purchase, in your currency and including the taxes of your country (for example, VAT). What each plan includes is described on the app's Subscription screen.

3. Free trial

3.1 Free Plus trial (no unexpected charges). If you are on the Free plan, you can try Plus for free for 30 days from the Subscription screen. This trial is offered directly by Capturia, not by the store: no payment method is requested and nothing is ever charged when it ends. When it ends, you automatically go back to the Free plan without doing anything; your data stays on your phone, but the Free plan limits apply again. If you want to keep Plus, you can buy it at any time.

The Plus trial can only be used once per account and once per device. To prevent abuse, an encrypted identifier of the device is stored when you activate it (see the privacy policy). Capturia may withdraw or change this trial in the future, without affecting trials already in progress.

3.2 Free Premium trial. When offered, anyone who has already bought Plus and subscribes to Premium for the first time can enjoy a free trial period (for example, one month). This trial is managed by the store: when it ends, the subscription becomes paid and is charged automatically unless you cancel it in the store before it ends. If you cancel, you go back to Plus when it ends. Each person can only enjoy this trial once, according to the store's rules.

4. Premium renewal and cancellation

The Premium subscription renews automatically every month and is charged at the start of each period until you cancel it.

You can cancel at any time from the store (Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; App Store → Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or from Subscription → Manage subscription in the app. After cancelling, you keep Premium until the end of the period already paid and you will not be charged again. If you have Plus, you go back to Plus; otherwise, to Free.

Deleting the app or your Capturia account does not cancel the subscription: you must cancel it in the store.

5. Price changes

Capturia may review its prices, in particular periodically (for example, once a year), to reflect changes in the cost of the service (servers, storage, store fees), changes in taxes or inflation, or when significant features are added.

  • Premium subscription: if the price goes up, we will notify you at least 30 days in advance through the store and/or the app. The new price will apply from the first renewal after that notice period and never to a period you have already paid for. If you do not agree, you can cancel the subscription before it applies, at no cost. Where the store requires you to expressly accept the new price and you do not, the subscription will end at the end of the current period.
  • Plus (one-time payment): if you have already bought it, you will not be charged again. Price changes only affect new purchases.
  • Offers and discounts: promotional prices are temporary and do not entitle you to keep them after the promotion ends, unless the offer itself states otherwise.

Price reductions may be applied without prior notice.

6. What "forever" means for Plus

Plus is paid once and is kept for as long as you have your Capturia account and the app remains in operation. The features and limits of each plan may evolve over time (for example, to add improvements); if the benefits of a paid plan were ever significantly reduced, we would tell you in advance.

7. Right of withdrawal and refunds

As a consumer in the European Union, you generally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase. However, since this is digital content activated at the moment of purchase, by paying you expressly request that Plus or Premium be activated immediately and acknowledge that you therefore lose your right of withdrawal (Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU and Article 103.m of Spanish Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007).

Refunds are handled by the store according to its own policies (for example, Google Play allows requests in some cases within the first 48 hours). If you have any problem with a purchase, write to us and we will help you. This does not limit the rights the law grants you if the service does not work as described.

8. Purchases and your account

Your purchase is linked to your Capturia account and to your store account. If you change phone or reinstall the app, sign in with the same account and use Restore purchases on the Subscription screen.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. If the change is significant, we will notify you in the app with reasonable notice; changes do not apply to periods you have already paid for. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.
